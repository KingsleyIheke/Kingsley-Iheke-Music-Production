Music production

Music production is the best activity i love the doing, in case you do not understand what it means, it is the process of or turning ideas into a finished song bringing together the rhythm, melody, recording and most importantly, the creativity. it gives me joy how different sounds can work together to create a feeling or story.

From an Idea To a song

a song can begin with something simple, a hummed melody, a drum pattern, a few chords or line of lyrics. The producer develops that starting point by choosing sounds, recording performances and arranging the different parts.

Tools Behind the Music

Music producers use tools like Microphone, headphones, keyboards and recording software. software like logic pro allows producers to record, mix and sample sounds.

Main Stages of Music production

  1. Developing the musical idea
  2. Recording instruments and vocals
  3. Arranging the song
  4. Editing and mixing
  5. Mastering the finished track
